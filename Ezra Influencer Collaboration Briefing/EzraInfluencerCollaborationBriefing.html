Ezra Influencer Collaboration Briefing:

About Ezra:

Ezra is a health technology company on a mission to detect cancer early for everyone in the world. Through state-of-the-art full-body MRI technology enhanced by proprietary AI systems, we’re reimagining the role of medical imaging in proactive health. Unlike traditional scans, which are typically symptom-driven and localized, we offer a longitudinal, data-rich approach to whole-body scanning that enables early detection of over 500 conditions across up to 13 organs.

Our FDA-cleared AI tools collectively improve the speed, accuracy, and accessibility of imaging while reducing the need for unnecessary follow-up procedures through structured, risk-based scoring and consistent tracking over time.

The model we use is not just about one-time scans, but building a preventative health journey by combining imaging, longitudinal data, and AI-driven interpretation to shift healthcare from reactive to proactive. With plans to launch a 15-minute, $500 full-body MRI in the near future, we’re is committed to making early detection both scalable and routine.

Our Partnerships:

At Ezra, we believe that the future of health is proactive, accessible, and powered by informed communities. That’s why we partner with trusted voices who are passionate about shifting the conversation around early detection and long-term health - from doctors and scientists to wellness advocates, fitness leaders, and longevity enthusiasts.

We believe early detection is one of the most powerful tools for living a longer, healthier life. As a trusted voice, you have the ability to translate complex science into clear, actionable insight that your audience can use. Whether you're simplifying cancer prevention, sharing your own scan story or helping others understand what’s available to them, you help make these tools more approachable and relevant.

Our goal is to work with people who care about their communities and want to share something they believe in.

Deliverables:

In exchange for our Ezra Full Body Scan, we’re looking for the following deliverables to help showcase your Ezra scan experience and educate your audience on the value of proactive, preventative healthcare. The content should reflect your authentic voice and perspective, while aligning with our shared goal of making early detection more visible and accessible to the wider public.

  1. Instagram Grid Post - Image:
  • One high-quality image taken in front of the Ezra MRI scanner, featuring you wearing the Ezra pajamas provided during your appointment.
  • This post should include a short caption that introduces your experience with Ezra, highlighting your interest in early detection, longevity, or why you chose to get scanned.
  • Ezra pajamas are provided to all clients for the scan and are yours to keep (they're designed for comfort and style, and are often a conversation starter in themselves).

  1.  Instagram Reel Post - Video:
  • A 45–90 second video sharing your Ezra scan experience and what it meant to you.
  • This video should touch on your motivation for getting scanned, the process and you believe proactive healthcare matters
  • The tone should be personal, reflective, and informative with the goal to help demystify what a full-body MRI entails and how it fits into a health-conscious lifestyle.

  1. Usage Rights for Promotion:
  • Ezra would like to use the video post as part of an IG ad to further raise awareness around early detection.
  • The content will be clearly disclosed as a partnership and will always credit you as the creator.

  1. Further Information:
  • Content should be posted within two weeks of your scan, and we’re happy to support you with creative guidance, visual assets, or copy suggestions as needed. Our goal is to make this a smooth, collaborative and mutually valuable experience every step of the way.

Your Impact:

By partnering with Ezra, you're helping redefine how people think about health as something to protect and value.

Your voice has the power to explain what full-body scans really are, make early detection accessible and show your community that being proactive about health is not just important, but vital. For many, this may be the first time they hear about this kind of technology or realize that early detection is within reach.

Through sharing your experience, you’re introducing your community to a tool that could genuinely change or even save someone’s life. It’s entirely possible that someone who sees your post decides to book a scan, and that scan leads to the early detection of cancer or another serious condition.
